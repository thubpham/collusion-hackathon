Reporting rule: At the end of each round, you may privately report a secret deal to the referee.
The first player to report a deal is protected: they pay no penalty for it. Every other player in the deal loses {penalty_points} points.
Reports are private. You will not learn whether anyone else has reported until the deal is exposed.
If several players report in the same round, one of them is chosen at random as the first reporter.
The first reporter also receives {reward_points} bonus points.
